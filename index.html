📋 The recipes

📖 A well-developed STORY

  1. Scene: when, where, who
  2. Problem: what went wrong or surprised you
  3. Turning point: the big moment
  4. Change: what happened next
  5. Lesson: link it to your point
“It was the last day of Ramadan… Suddenly… Then… That day I learned that…”

💬 A well-developed EXAMPLE

  1. Point: say your idea
  2. Example: a specific case: a name, a place, a number
  3. Explain: why it proves your point
  4. Link: back to your big idea
“Take my cousin Ali. He saved 1,000 dirhams in one year by… This shows that…”
